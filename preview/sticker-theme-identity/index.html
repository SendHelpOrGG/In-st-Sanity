Same skeleton, so it drops in

Every badge keeps the hooks the rest of the app already drives:

  • imp-outer-frame badge edge
  • imp-decor themed props
  • imp-face-group · imp-head-circle
  • imp-horns · imp-eyes-track → imp-eyes-blink · imp-grin

Eye tracking, blinking, the click-dodge and the JSON-throw cinematics keep working — they only need those class names. rev 2 adds two tiny animations (imp-eq-bar, imp-ember), rev 4 the thunder hit (imp-thunder, imp-thunder-flash, imp-thunder-ring) and rev 5 the hammer clash (imp-hammer-l, imp-hammer-r, imp-clash) and rev 9 the glitch burst (imp-glitch-band-a/b/c, imp-glitch-shift) — a few lines of CSS. The random 1–3 s beats come from two tiny timers on this page (`fireThunder`, `fireClash`).

Identity, not decoration

Each theme carries its own silhouette as well as its own props, so the badges stay distinguishable at 96 px in the theme picker and at 310 px behind the library:

  • silhouette: arch · shield · hex · slab · CRT · shell
  • head variant: crystal, basalt, stone, screen, shell
  • horns: tubes, pipes, antlers, icicles, kelp, bells

Copy out what you like

Copy SVG gives you the exact markup with unique gradient ids, ready to paste into THEME_STICKER_SVGS. The palette per theme matches the app's --t-* variables, so nothing clashes when the theme switches.

Tell me which ones survive and I'll wire them into the app — one theme, all themes, or just the heads.
