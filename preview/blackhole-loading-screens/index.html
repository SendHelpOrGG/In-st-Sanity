The black hole, five ways

The same idea every time: the imp, his two arms, and a black hole opened between his palms. The shipped boot screen already does this once — these five keep that scene intact and push one part of it harder: the camera, the hands, the jaw, the light, or the clap. Nothing here is wired into the app.

The wordmark uses the boot screen's own glitch: the RGB slices, the skew and the 0.65–3.4 s bursts are ported straight from lsBrandGlitch.
